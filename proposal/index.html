What's built today
A complete workflow tracker, built for valuation engineers
📋

5-Stage Project Pipeline

Lead → Survey → Drafting → Report → Review. Every project moves through a defined, trackable workflow.

✅

Digital Checklists

Bank-specific checklists (Home Loan, etc.) submitted digitally per stage. No paper, no lost forms.

👥

Staff Assignment & Occupancy

Assign surveyors, draughtsmen, and report staff per project. See who is free, light, or overloaded.

📁

File Uploads Per Stage

Attach survey photos, drawings, and documents directly to the relevant project stage.

📊

Live Dashboard

See all projects by stage at a glance. Stage distribution bar, staff occupancy heat map.

🔐

Role-Based Access

Admin sees everything. Staff see only their assigned tasks. Secure, cloud-hosted, always available.
